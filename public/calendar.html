How activity is counted

All dates use America/Chicago, including daylight saving time. Registry additions count unique publicly eligible Cybercabs by the date added to the registry, rather than their first sighting. Sightings use the date spotted and the existing approved public photo rules. Public photos expire after 30 days; older sighting totals cannot be reconstructed reliably and are unavailable. Camera captures use actual detection times; duplicate camera/time captures are prevented by the database.

DMV totals include Cybercab and Model Y VINs first observed after the initial baseline, only on days with a successful Texas DMV snapshot. The source does not publish official registration dates. Missing polls and the baseline are unavailable, and approximate imported history is excluded.

Combined score: each available category’s count is log-scaled against its 90th percentile positive daily count for the selected year, capped at 100, then averaged with equal weight. Only available categories contribute; partial days are marked with a dashed border. Scores are relative to the selected year and update as data arrives. Individual category colors use positive-day quantiles so unusually large days do not obscure smaller activity. A confirmed zero means no qualifying records, not proof no Cybercabs were active.
