    HOW DISCOVERY WORKS

    1. 1

      Ride in one

      Add your Robotaxi receipt. The first counted ride in a vehicle earns its discovery.

    2. 2

      Or spot one first

      Submit a photo sighting of a Cybercab that isn't in the registry yet. Once it's approved, the discovery is yours.

    3. 3

      It counts once it's public

      Only vehicles in the public registry count, and the earliest ride or sighting wins.

    Spotters are listed by name and photo. Prefer not to be? Turn off Community leaderboard & public profile on your Profile to appear as “Private spotter”.
