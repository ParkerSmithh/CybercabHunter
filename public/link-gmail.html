Trouble?

Confirmation not showing?

Wait a minute and refresh. Gmail can be slow to send it. If it still hasn't come, check that you pasted the whole address in step 2.

No receipts arriving?

In Gmail settings, check that your forwarding address shows as confirmed under Forwarding and POP/IMAP, and that your filter is still listed under Filters and Blocked Addresses. If you got a new address on this page, the filter needs the new one.

Bring your past rides

Filters only forward new mail, so receipts from before you set this up need one manual pass.Receipts from rides you've already taken can be sent the same way, once.

  1. In Gmail, search for to find your old receipts.
  2. Open each receipt and forward it to your address.
  3. Send them one at a time. Gmail's Forward as attachment option bundles emails in a way we can't read.

Not sure if you already sent one? Send it anyway. Duplicates are caught automatically, so a ride is never counted twice.
