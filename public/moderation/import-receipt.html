Import Receipt

Adds a Tesla Robotaxi ride to the chosen rider's Rider Data through the normal receipt pipeline. A plate the registry hasn't seen becomes a private registry vehicle. Nothing is made public here: to publish it, verify the vehicle, enter its VIN and use Approve Cybercab in Registry Vehicles.

The ride is added to this rider's Rider Data. Only riders with a display name can be chosen.

    • .eml: the Tesla receipt email saved as a file (in Gmail: open the email, ⋮ → Download message). Best: the sender and headers help confirm it.
    • .txt: the receipt email's text copied into a plain-text file.
    • Up to 25 files, about 2 MB each. PDFs and screenshots aren't supported.
