Import Receipt

Adds a Tesla Robotaxi ride to your own Rider Data through the normal receipt pipeline. A plate the registry hasn't seen becomes a private registry vehicle. Nothing is made public here: to publish it, verify the vehicle, enter its VIN and use Approve Cybercab in Registry Vehicles.

  • .eml — the Tesla receipt email saved as a file (in Gmail: open the email, ⋮ → Download message). Best: the sender and headers help confirm it.
  • .txt — the receipt email's text copied into a plain-text file.
  • Up to 25 files, about 2 MB each. PDFs and screenshots aren't supported.
