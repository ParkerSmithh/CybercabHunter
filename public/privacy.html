Privacy

Cybercab Hunter is a community tracker for Tesla Robotaxi rides and vehicles. This page explains what information it uses and how.

Your account

If you sign in with Google, Cybercab Hunter receives your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile picture, and uses them to create and recognize your account. Normal sign-in does not give Cybercab Hunter any access to your Gmail.

Ride receipts

Your rides come from Tesla Robotaxi receipt emails. A receipt can reach Cybercab Hunter by being forwarded to your private receipt address, by being imported, or — only if you choose to connect it — directly from Gmail (below).

From each receipt, Cybercab Hunter keeps the ride details it needs for Rider Data: the ride date, pickup and drop-off times, pickup and drop-off locations, distance, duration, fare and the vehicle’s license plate. The email itself is read to extract those details and is not stored. Your name and payment details that appear in a receipt are not kept.

Optional Gmail connection

  • What it is. From Rider Data you can choose to connect the Gmail account you signed in with. This is separate from signing in and asks Google for read-only Gmail access (the “gmail.readonly” permission).
  • Why. So your Tesla Robotaxi receipts are added to Rider Data automatically, without forwarding them yourself.
  • What is read. About every 10 minutes, Cybercab Hunter searches your Gmail for emails whose subject contains “Robotaxi Ride Receipt”, and reads only those matching emails. When you first connect, the search covers the last 90 days. Other emails are not downloaded.
  • What is kept. The ride details listed above for receipts that are valid Tesla Robotaxi receipts; your Gmail address; Gmail’s internal identifiers for the receipt emails already processed (so they are not imported twice — kept for up to 120 days); and Gmail’s internal position marker for your mailbox. Email contents are not stored.
  • How the authorization is protected. The long-lived authorization Google issues is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key used only for Gmail. Short-lived access tokens are used in memory and never stored. Tokens are never shown in the website or written to logs.
  • How to stop it. Choose “Unlink Gmail” on Rider Data: Cybercab Hunter asks Google to revoke the authorization, deletes the stored authorization and sync records, and stops reading your Gmail. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account’s third-party access page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Rides already imported stay in Rider Data until you remove them there.

What is public

Cybercab Hunter publishes a public registry of Robotaxi vehicles. A vehicle appears there only after a moderator has reviewed and approved it, and the public pages show vehicle-level information (such as the license plate and ride counts) derived from receipts.

Community profile: unless you turn it off, your display name, username, profile picture (for Google sign-in, the picture from your Google account), bio, the month you joined, the public registry vehicles you discovered, and counts from your rides (how many rides you have recorded, how many different Cybercabs you have ridden in, and the cities you have ridden in, with the number of rides in each) are shown on the Community leaderboard and on your public profile page. Only counts and city names are shown, never fares, dates, times, addresses or the plates of vehicles you rode in. This is on by default for every account. Turn off “Community leaderboard & public profile” on your Profile page at any time to appear only as “Private spotter” (a rank and count, with no name or picture).

Cybercab reviews: a review you post on the Community page (its stars, text, photos and the vehicle it is about) and your comments on reviews are public. They show your display name, username and profile picture, unless you have turned off “Community leaderboard & public profile”, in which case they show as “Anonymous”. Location and other metadata are removed from review photos before they are stored. Who liked a review is never shown, only how many people did. You can delete your own reviews and comments at any time.

Your email, receipt contents, fares, ride dates and times, and addresses are never shown publicly.

Rider Data lets you remove individual rides or all of your rides.
