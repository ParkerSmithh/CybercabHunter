This is permanent and can't be undone.

Deleting your account removes:

  • your account, profile, username and Community listing
  • your Google, Tesla and Gmail connections (Gmail access is revoked)
  • your ride history, receipts and receipt forwarding address
  • your submissions and sightings, including their photos

Vehicles in the public registry stay listed; your name is removed from them.
