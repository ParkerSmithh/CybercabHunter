How this is estimated

The wait is the time to reach the nearest free car. With free cars spread over the service area, the nearest one is on average 0.5 ÷ √(free cars per square mile) away in a straight line. That distance times a road detour factor, over an average speed, plus a minute to dispatch, gives the wait. The range runs from the best to the worst end of each assumption below.
